# KleenSlate - Start Fresh Every Day

Tired of endless task lists that feel more stressful than helpful?  
**KleenSlate** is a beautifully simple to-do list app that helps you reset, refocus, and reclaim your day - one clean slate at a time.

With a focus on **clarity**, **calm**, and **control**, KleenSlate helps you organize your tasks without the overwhelm. Each morning starts with a fresh list. No clutter. No guilt. Just you and what truly matters today.

---

## ✨ Why KleenSlate?

Because productivity doesn’t need to be noisy.  
Because starting over is powerful.  
Because sometimes, less *really* is more.
